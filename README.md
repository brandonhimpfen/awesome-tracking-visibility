## Container & Freight Tracking APIs

Data platforms, vessel tracking APIs, and ocean container status aggregators providing real-time milestone events.

- [VesselFinder API](https://www.vesselfinder.com/) — Global AIS vessel tracking data service delivering real-time positioning, port calls, and vessel master data.
- [MarineTraffic API](https://www.marinetraffic.com/) — Comprehensive maritime tracking engine providing vessel positions, port congestion metrics, and route history.
- [ShipsGo API](https://shipsgo.com/) — Container tracking platform offering real-time ocean freight status notifications and delay alerts.
- [Container-Tracking-Parser](https://github.com/shipping-data) — Open-source library for parsing raw carrier container status codes into standardized milestone schemas.

## Asset & Asset-Tracking IoT

Open-source hardware protocols, low-power telemetry modules, and cellular/satellite tracking frameworks for equipment and cargo units.

- [Open-Asset-Tracker](https://github.com/open-asset-tracker) — Open hardware and firmware framework for low-power GPS and cellular asset tracking nodes.
- [Thingstream IoT Service](https://github.com/u-blox) — Global connectivity and positioning protocols designed for battery-constrained asset tracking devices.
- [LoRaWAN Asset Telemetry](https://github.com/TheThingsNetwork) — Open schemas and device payload decoders for tracking assets over community LoRaWAN networks.
- [BLE-Beacon-Tracking-Engine](https://github.com/beacon-tracker) — Lightweight positioning library for locating tagged warehouse equipment using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) gateways.

## Cold Chain Telemetry & Sensing

Hardware blueprints, logging engines, and threshold alert modules for monitoring temperature-sensitive pharmaceuticals and perishable food cargo.

- [Open-Cold-Chain-Logger](https://github.com/enviro-diy) — Open-source temperature and humidity logging node designed for cold chain verification during transit.
- [ColdChain-Data-Standard](https://github.com/openag) — Unified data schema for recording environmental sensor metrics, trip events, and excursion logs.
- [HACCP Telemetry Rules Engine](https://github.com/haccp-compliance) — Processing pipeline for flagging thermal excursions and calculating shelf-life decay rates in transit.

## Predictive ETA & Visibility Platforms

Machine learning algorithms, arrival prediction engines, and multi-modal shipment visibility platforms.

### Open Source Engines

- [Open-ETA-Calculator](https://github.com/routing-py/routing-py) — Algorithmic engine for estimating freight arrival times using historical transit matrices and spatial data.
- [Cargo-Event-Hub](https://github.com/logistics-events) — Microservice architecture for unifying disparate carrier tracking webhooks into a single real-time event stream.

### Commercial Visibility Platforms

- [FourKites Developer Hub](https://www.fourkites.com/) — Enterprise real-time supply chain visibility platform offering multi-modal predictive tracking APIs.
- [Overhaul Intelligence](https://over-haul.com/) — Supply chain risk management and visibility platform integrating real-time cargo security monitoring.
